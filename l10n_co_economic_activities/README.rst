Business Need
-------------

In Colombia, every company must declare its CIIU economic activity codes
(Clasificación Industrial Internacional Uniforme) as defined by the DANE
and required by the DIAN for electronic invoicing and withholding tax
calculations.

Approach
--------

This module adds an ``l10n.co.economic.activity`` model with CIIU codes
and extends ``res.partner`` with fields for primary, secondary, and
other economic activities. Data is pre-loaded with official CIIU codes
used by the DIAN.

Useful Information
------------------

- **Depends on:** ``base``, ``contacts``, ``account``,
  ``base_address_extended``
- **Works well with:** ``l10n_co_withholding``,
  ``l10n_co_electronic_invoice``
- **Suggested setup:** Required for Colombian electronic invoicing and
  withholding tax configuration

Usage
=====

To use this module, you need to:

- Go to *Contacts* > select a partner
- Set the country to *Colombia*
- In the *Accounting* tab, the *Actividades Económicas Colombia* group
  appears
- Select the primary, secondary, and other economic activities from the
  CIIU catalog
- These codes are used for electronic invoicing and withholding tax
  calculations
